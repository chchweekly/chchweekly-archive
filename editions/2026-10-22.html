What's happening this weekend? 👀

Friday, 23rd Oct

🏀 Mainland Pouākai v Tokomanawa Queens

7:30pm | Parakiore Recreation and Sport Centre

WNBA talent comes to Christchurch this Friday as the Tokomanawa Queens take on the Mainland Pouākai! Don't miss your chance to see world-class women's basketball talent go head-to-head.

🎺 Big Band Jazz at Fat Eddie's

From 4:00pm | Fat Eddie's - Free

The Christchurch Big Band Jazz Festival brings live music to Fat Eddie's! Enjoy fantastic jazz performances alongside pizza, drinks and a lively atmosphere in the heart of the city.

🎶 Te Reo Tautito

7:00pm | Lyttelton Arts Factory - Koha

Experience modern jazz, R&B and pop performed in te reo Māori, accompanied by Kāi Tahu artist George French creating a live painting inspired by the music.

Saturday, 24th Oct

🎷 Christchurch Big Band Jazz Parade

1:30pm | Central Christchurch - Free

The central city comes alive with live jazz as musicians and dancers parade from Fat Eddie's through Cathedral Square towards Tūranga. A fun, free event for the whole family.

🍷 Ferrymead Heritage Wine & Food Festival

10:00am–5:00pm | Ferrymead Heritage Park - Ticketed, R18

Meet winemakers from across New Zealand, discover new favourites and enjoy local food in Ferrymead's historic village. Special experiences include guided masterclasses and wine tastings aboard heritage trams.

🌳 Avon Bonsai Society Spring Show

From 10:00am | The Kiosk, Botanic Gardens - Free

Discover the fascinating art of growing miniature trees, with beautiful bonsai on display throughout the weekend. Meet local enthusiasts, learn about the craft and vote for your favourite tree.

🛍️ Showgrounds Twilight Vintage Market

4:00pm–9:00pm | Canterbury Agricultural Park - Free

Browse vintage clothing, retro collectibles, antiques, handcrafted goods and more at this twilight market. Enjoy food stalls and discover unique finds from local vendors.

🌱 Annual Labour Weekend Plant Sale

From 9:30am | Our Lady of Victories Church Hall - Free entry

Get your garden ready for spring with a huge selection of affordable plants, including vegetables, herbs, flowers, fruit trees and indoor plants. Proceeds support the upkeep of local parish gardens.

Sunday, 25th Oct

🌸 Orton Bradley Park Spring Fair

10:00am–4:00pm | Orton Bradley Park, Charteris Bay - Gate entry

Head out to Charteris Bay for a family day featuring market stalls, live music, pony rides, vintage machinery and beautiful spring gardens. Explore the walking tracks and enjoy the rhododendrons in full bloom.

🎲 Heritage Fun Zone

From 11:00am | The Arts Centre, South Quad - Free

Step back in time with traditional games from the 1800s! Try your hand at skittles, hoops, spinning tops, egg-and-spoon races and more. A fun way for the whole family to experience a little history.

🚒 Ferrymead First Responders Open Day

From 10:00am | Ferrymead Heritage Park - Admission applies

Meet the people who help keep Canterbury safe! Get up close with emergency vehicles, watch live demonstrations and enjoy hands-on activities with firefighters, police, rescue teams and more.

🎭 Performing Arts Precinct Open Day

10:00am–2:00pm | Central Christchurch - Free

Go behind the scenes at some of Christchurch's best performing arts venues. The Court Theatre, Isaac Theatre Royal, The Piano and Tūranga will host free performances, workshops and activities throughout the day.

🛍️ Cheap Charlies Market

From 11:00am | Near One New Zealand Stadium - Free entry

Heading to the rugby league? Stop by Cheap Charlies Market, just 150 metres from the stadium! Enjoy local food, drinks from $5 and a relaxed atmosphere before or after the big games.

🪔 Diwali 2026

2:00pm–10:00pm | QEII Park, New Brighton - Free

Celebrate the Festival of Lights with traditional Indian dancing, Bollywood performances, delicious food and market stalls. The festivities continue into the evening with colourful light displays and fireworks.

What's on next week 📅

Monday, 26th Oct

🏉 NPC Grand Final: Canterbury v Northland

2:05pm | One New Zealand Stadium - From $15.30

Canterbury take on Northland in the NPC Grand Final this Labour Day! Get behind the red and blacks as they battle for the national title in a huge afternoon of rugby at ourr new stadium.

🎺 Big Band Picnic on the Archery Lawn

10:00am–4:00pm | Christchurch Botanic Gardens - Free

Make the most of Labour Day with a relaxing picnic in the Botanic Gardens. Enjoy performances from big bands throughout the day, with plenty of jazz, swing music and food trucks.

🎨 The Arts Centre Sculpture Festival

10:00am–5:00pm | The Arts Centre - Free

Last chance to explore more than 300 works from over 70 artists, featuring contemporary jewellery, sculptural objects and outdoor installations. Wander through the Great Hall and North Quad to discover some incredible local and national talent.

Tuesday, 27th Oct

🏛️ Parihaka History Talk & Discussion

7:30pm | Northcity Church, Northcote - Free

Learn about the history of Parihaka, the peaceful Taranaki settlement invaded by Crown forces in 1881, and its enduring message of non-violent resistance and reconciliation.

Wednesday, 28th Oct

🎭 Bride of Hyde

7:00pm | Little Andromeda - Ticketed

Get into the Halloween spirit with this chilling historical horror production. Two sisters find themselves pursued by a mysterious monster in a theatrical story from the creators of Coffin Ship.

😂 James Nokise: One Night Rant

8:30pm | Little Andromeda - Ticketed

Award-winning comedian James Nokise brings his fast-paced political satire to Christchurch, taking aim at politicians, policies and everything in between.

Thursday, 29th Oct

🔧 Cashmere Repair Café

5:30pm–7:00pm | St Augustine's Church, Cashmere - Free

Give your broken belongings a second life! Bring along clothing, small appliances, bikes or other items and get help repairing them from friendly local volunteers.

🎃 Halloween Beats By Bingo

7:00pm–9:00pm | Robbie's Lichfield - Free

A Halloween twist on musical bingo! Test your music knowledge with spooky tunes, throwback hits, prizes and hilarious mini-games, hosted by comedian Taylor Ruddle.

Local headlines 📰

A couple of stories you might've missed.

✈️ Christchurch Airport named NZ's Large Airport of the Year

Christchurch Airport has taken home two national awards, recognising its growth, new international connections and sustainability efforts, including the recently completed Kōwhai Park solar farm.

🚐 New monitoring trial targets freedom camping

Christchurch City Council is introducing a six-month trial using automatic number- plate monitoring to identify freedom campers breaking local rules. The trial begins today, 22 October.

🏟️ Big sporting week tipped to bring $10m boost to Christchurch

A packed week of international cricket, netball and rugby league, alongside the NPC Grand Final, is expected to bring more than 68,000 spectators to Christchurch's sporting venues and inject an estimated $10 million into the local economy.
