What's happening this weekend? 👀

Friday, 16th Oct

🏛️ Christchurch Heritage Festival 2026

16–26 Oct | Various locations across Christchurch

Discover Christchurch's history with 11 days of guided walks, historic building tours, exhibitions, talks and open days. Plenty of opportunities to explore familiar places and uncover stories you might never have heard before.

🏠 Canterbury Home Show

10:00am | Wolfbrook Arena

The Canterbury Home Show returns for three days of renovation inspiration, home improvement ideas and exhibitors showcasing products and services for around the house.

🚴 Biketober – Friday Night Bikes

5:45pm | Bridge of Remembrance

Take to the streets for a social evening ride as part of Biketober. A chance to explore Christchurch by bike and meet other local cyclists along the way.

🎼 NZ Opera – The Pearl Fishers

7:30pm | Isaac Theatre Royal

New Zealand Opera brings Bizet's classic story of friendship, loyalty and desire to Christchurch, accompanied by the Christchurch Symphony Orchestra.

🎵 Adam McGrath & Gary McCormick

7:30pm | Lyttelton Arts Factory

Musician Adam McGrath and broadcaster Gary McCormick team up for an evening of Kiwi songs, stories, poetry and plenty of laughs.

🪩 Michael Jackson Club Night

8:00pm | A Rolling Stone - R18

Dance the night away to Michael Jackson's biggest hits at this special tribute club night. Expect plenty of classics, throwback favourites and a dance floor full of MJ fans.

Saturday, 17th Oct

🌱 Plantarama

9:00am | Cashmere Hills Presbyterian Church

Stock up for spring at the annual Plantarama sale, with native plants, perennials, vegetable plants and plenty of other gardening goodies.

⛵ Model Yacht Sailing Open Day

10:00am | Christchurch Model Yacht Club, North Hagley Park

Ever wanted to try sailing a model yacht? Head to Victoria Lake, where club members will help visitors have a go at steering radio-controlled yachts.

🌷 Avebury House Spring Fair

10:00am | Avebury House, Richmond

A spring day out with vintage market stalls, food, Devonshire teas, activities for the kids and a chance to explore historic Avebury House.

11:00am | Tūranga - Free

Discover new hobbies, meet local groups and try activities including tai chi, dancing and more. Over 20 exhibitors come together for this free event celebrating life at every age.

7:30pm | Parakiore Recreation and Sport Centre

Canterbury's professional women's basketball team takes on the Southern Hoiho in a country-themed game, complete with country music, line dancing and plenty of action on court.

Sunday, 18th Oct

🎪 Governors Bay Community Fete

10:00am | Allandale Domain - Free

A lovely excuse for a trip over the hill. Enjoy craft and gift stalls, live music, food, Devonshire teas and games for the kids at this community fete.

🎮 The Geek Market

10:00am | Richmond Working Men's Club

A market dedicated to all things geeky! Browse collectibles, toys, memorabilia and other treasures, with plenty to discover for collectors and curious visitors alike.

🔭 Observatory Tower Guided Tours

🔤 Competitive Scrabble Have-A-Go Day

1:00pm | Parakiore Recreation and Sport Centre - Free

Think you're handy with a few letters? Join the Christchurch Scrabble Club for an afternoon of games, tips and prizes. Beginners are welcome!

🍔 Food Truck Alley

4:00pm | Mt Pleasant Community Centre - Free entry

An afternoon of food trucks, live music and family entertainment, with pony rides, face painting, a bouncy castle and plenty to keep the kids busy.

🎻 NZSO Elegance

7:30pm | Christchurch Town Hall

The New Zealand Symphony Orchestra returns to Christchurch, joined by acclaimed pianist Isata Kanneh-Mason for an evening featuring music by Mozart, Brahms and Andrée.

Local Headlines 📰

A couple of stories you might've missed 👇

🛒 Costco eyes first Christchurch store near airport

US retail giant Costco is reportedly looking to open its first South Island store on land between Memorial Avenue and Avonhead Road. Council has been asked for feedback on a fast-track application, although the development hasn't been approved yet.

💰 Christchurch rates rises could drop to 4%

Mayor Phil Mauger says Council staff have found a way to target rates increases of 6%, 5% and 4% over the next three years without cutting services. Councillors will need to stick to those targets for the plan to work.

🏟️ What's next for Christchurch's old rugby stadium?

The Government is reviewing planning rules for the former Apollo Projects Stadium site in Addington, exploring whether changes could make it easier to redevelop the land for housing or other uses.

🐦 Fantails return as Banks Peninsula stream gets a makeover

A $45,000 restoration project at Orton Bradley Park is helping improve water quality and restore native habitats, with growing numbers of pīwakawaka (fantails) spotted around Te Wharau Stream.

🐶 Christchurch's most popular dog name revealed

Luna has overtaken Charlie as Christchurch's favourite dog name, with 421 registrations compared to Charlie's 420. And among the city's more unusual names? There are 30 Kevins, 24 Trevors and 15 Garys!
