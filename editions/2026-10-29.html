What's happening this weekend? 👀

Friday, 30th Oct

🎃 Halloween Mākete at The Arts Centre

4:00pm–9:00pm | The Arts Centre, Market Square - Free

Get into the Halloween spirit with two evenings of spooky market fun! Explore over 60 local makers and creatives, enjoy delicious food trucks, face painting, live performances and plenty of Halloween decorations. Also running Saturday evening!

🎶 Louis Baker - Trouble Album Release Tour

From 7:00pm | The Loons, Lyttelton - Ticketed, R18

Acclaimed Kiwi soul musician Louis Baker brings his new album Trouble to Lyttelton! Enjoy an intimate evening of soulful vocals, rich melodies and favourites from across his catalogue as part of his nationwide album release tour.

🎭 All Hallows' Improv

7:30pm | The Piano - From $10

Get ready for a frightfully funny evening as Impulse Theatre brings its Halloween comedy special to The Piano! Expect spooky stories, hilarious improvised scenes and plenty of unexpected twists. Dress up for a chance to win costume prizes.

⛸️ Halloween Ice Skating

7:30pm–9:30pm | Alpine Ice - $13

Hit the ice for a spooky skating session with Halloween music, costumes and prizes! Dress in your scariest outfit and enjoy a fun night on the rink. There's also a family-friendly session on Saturday afternoon.

Saturday, 31st Oct

🏉 Black Ferns v France

7:10pm | One New Zealand Stadium - Ticketed

The Black Ferns take on France in their first Test at Christchurch's new stadium! Get behind the team for a huge evening of international women's rugby, with a historic NZ Under-21 v Australia NextGen match kicking off at 4:30pm.

🚗 Take Charge Electric Vehicle Expo

10:00am–5:00pm | Te Pae Christchurch - Free

Curious about electric vehicles? Explore 30 electric cars from 18 brands, including Tesla, BYD, BMW and more. Compare models, discover the latest technology and chat with exhibitors at this free one-day showcase.

🐶 Common Ground Halloween Dog Parade

From 10:30am | 91 Estuary Road, South Brighton - Free

Christchurch's four-legged friends are getting into the Halloween spirit! Watch dogs parade in their funniest and spookiest costumes, with prizes for the best dressed. Bring your own pup or just come along to enjoy the fun.

🧙 Maleficent's Park of Tricks & Treats

10:00am–8:00pm | Ferrymead Heritage Park - Ticketed

Ferrymead transforms into a Halloween wonderland for the whole family! Explore trick-or-treat stations, meet costumed characters and enjoy bouncy castles, face painting, market stalls and a Monster Mash Disco. Spookier surprises await in the evening!

🎡 Kaiapoi Halloween Twilight Carnival

2:00pm–8:30pm | Norman Kirk Park, Kaiapoi - Free entry

Kaiapoi comes alive with a Halloween carnival featuring market stalls, food trucks, fairground rides, costume competitions and a trick-or-treat trail. The celebrations wrap up with a spectacular fireworks display at 8:30pm!

🎆 Selwyn Fireworks Spooktacular

From 5:00pm | Foster Park, Rolleston - Ticketed

Get ready for a huge night of fireworks and family entertainment in Rolleston! Enjoy live performances, amusement rides, food stalls and activities before a dazzling fireworks display lights up the sky. Tickets must be purchased online.

🎻 CSO: The Dream of Gerontius

7:30pm | Christchurch Town Hall - Ticketed

The Christchurch Symphony Orchestra closes its 2026 season with Elgar's powerful choral masterpiece. Experience an unforgettable evening as the full orchestra, soloists and CSO Chorus bring this moving musical journey to life.

🎬 Halloween Drive-In: Scream (1996)

Gates 7:30pm | Canterbury Showgrounds - Ticketed, R16

Celebrate Halloween with a classic horror movie under the stars! Firefly Cinemas brings Scream to the big outdoor screen, complete with food trucks, spooky trivia, a costume competition and sound through your car radio. Film starts around 8:45pm.

Sunday, 1st Nov

🦆 Great Canterbury Duck Race

12:00pm–2:00pm | Bridge of Remembrance - Free to watch

Hundreds of rubber ducks take to the Avon River for a fun afternoon supporting Life Education Canterbury! Cheer on the business and community duck races, meet Harold the Giraffe and enjoy family activities while supporting a great local cause.

🛍️ Shabby Chic Market Day

11:00am–3:00pm | Rochester Villa, Woolston

Browse more than 40 stalls packed with vintage treasures, antiques, clothing, handcrafted goods and unique homewares at Christchurch's longest-running vintage market. Enjoy coffee, home baking and a relaxed atmosphere in the grounds of historic Rochester Villa. Bring cash!

💀 Día de Muertos - Day of the Dead

1:00pm–2:00pm | Tūranga, Cathedral Square - Free

Celebrate Mexico's colourful Day of the Dead tradition with live music, traditional dancing and creative activities. Discover a beautiful celebration of life, remembrance and culture, with performances from local Mexican and Latin American artists.

🎶 Sounds of Salaam: Music from Distant Lands

12:15pm–6:30pm | The Arts Centre, Great Hall - Free

Travel through the sounds of Asia and the Middle East with an afternoon of live music and cultural performances. Discover traditional instruments, captivating melodies and musical traditions from across the world in the beautiful Great Hall.

🪔 Selwyn Diwali Festival 2026

2:00pm–9:30pm | Lincoln Domain - Free

Celebrate the Festival of Lights in Lincoln with colourful cultural performances, live music, delicious Indian food, market stalls and family activities. The festivities continue into the evening with a spectacular fireworks finale!

What's on next week 📅

Monday, 2nd Nov

🎭 The Director's Circle: The Pirates of Penzance

6:00pm | The Court Theatre - $15

Go behind the scenes of The Court Theatre's upcoming production of The Pirates of Penzance! Join director Ross Gumbley for a look at how five performers bring this classic musical comedy to life, with plenty of theatrical tricks and surprises.

🎤 Open Mic Night: Music & Poetry

7:00pm–11:00pm | XCHC, Waltham - Free

Enjoy an evening of live music, poetry and local creativity at XCHC! Whether you'd like to perform or simply listen, everyone is welcome. Expect emerging local talent, a relaxed atmosphere and plenty of encouragement for first-time performers.

Tuesday, 3rd Nov

🎶 Christchurch Schools' Music Festival

7:00pm | Christchurch Town Hall - From $19

Thousands of Canterbury schoolchildren take to the stage for Te Hā Puoro, the annual Christchurch Schools' Music Festival! Enjoy an inspiring evening of massed choirs, orchestral performances and young musical talent. Running Tuesday through Friday.

🌿 Garden Life: Spring Gardening Talks

6:00pm–8:00pm | The Kiosk, Botanic Gardens - $10, members free

Get inspired for the growing season with gardening tips, expert speakers and friendly conversation. This month's session features the beautiful Fisherman's Bay Garden, seasonal advice from Oderings and creative garden ideas. Also running Wednesday afternoon.

🦁 The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe

7:00pm | Christ's College Old Boys' Theatre - Ticketed

Step through the wardrobe and into the magical world of Narnia! Students from Christ's College and Rangi Ruru bring C.S. Lewis's much-loved adventure to life, complete with the White Witch, Aslan and a world trapped in eternal winter. Running until Saturday.

Wednesday, 4th Nov

🎈 Up and Away: Theatre for Babies

10:00am & 11:00am | The Arts Centre, Cloisters Studio - Ticketed

A gentle theatre experience created especially for babies who aren't yet walking and their grown-ups. Enjoy live music, playful storytelling and sensory exploration in a cosy setting designed for little ones. Running until Sunday.

🎻 Wednesday at One: Leo Liu, Violin

1:10pm–2:00pm | Catholic Pro-Cathedral - $15

Take a musical break with talented violinist Leo Liu, performing a programme of classical and gypsy-inspired music. Enjoy beautiful live music in the Pro- Cathedral's impressive acoustics, with a light lunch available beforehand.

🎨 Te Ūranga: The Landing Place Exhibition

11:00am–5:00pm | Eastside Gallery, 388 Worcester St - Free

Discover artwork by 26 local artists celebrating the creative community of Ōtautahi Creative Spaces. Featuring a diverse range of artistic expressions, including works by first-time exhibitors, this new exhibition runs throughout November.

Thursday, 5th Nov

🎨 First Thursdays: Celebrating Change

5:00pm–9:00pm | Brighton Mall, New Brighton - Free

New Brighton comes alive after hours with live music, creative workshops, pop- up stalls, performances, food and activities! Explore local businesses staying open late, try something new and enjoy a lively community atmosphere. Workshops and have-a-go sessions are free or koha.

🕊️ Parihaka Day Commemorative Peace Service

1:00pm | World Peace Bell, Botanic Gardens - Free

Gather to remember the peaceful Taranaki settlement of Parihaka and the events of 5 November 1881. This commemorative service honours the legacy of Te Whiti- o-Rongomai and Tohu Kākahi, whose commitment to non-violent resistance continues to inspire today.

🎭 That Bloody Woman: The Musical

7:00pm | NASDA Theatre, Madras Street - From $20

Kate Sheppard's extraordinary story comes to life in this energetic rock musical! Follow the woman who helped lead New Zealand's suffrage movement, brought to the stage by NASDA's talented second-year students. Running until 12 November.

🎻 NZTrio: Reverie

7:30pm | The Piano - $10–$40

Acclaimed chamber ensemble NZTrio brings an evening of beautiful classical music to Christchurch. Enjoy works by Schumann, Bernstein and contemporary composers, including a new piece by New Zealand composer Karlo Margetić.

Local headlines 📰

A couple of stories you might've missed.

🐏 Runaway ram goes on rampage through Christchurch office

A runaway ram caused chaos in Addington over Labour Weekend, smashing through an office window and damaging furniture inside. The sheep had reportedly been on the loose for three days before police and animal control found it inside the building.

✈️ Christchurch welcomes first direct flights to Perth

Jetstar's first direct flight from Perth touched down in Christchurch on Tuesday, launching a new seasonal service operating three times a week. Direct Jetstar flights to Sydney have also returned, expanding travel options ahead of summer.

🎟️ Christchurch events deliver record $107m boost

Venues Ōtautahi has reported a record $107 million economic impact for the 2025/26 financial year, hosting 474 events and welcoming more than 930,000 visitors across its venues. The figures include just 10 weeks of operation at the new One New Zealand Stadium.
