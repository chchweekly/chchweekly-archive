What's happening this weekend? 👀

Friday, 9th Oct

🇫🇷 Akaroa French Festival

5:00pm | Akaroa Recreation Ground

Akaroa celebrates its French heritage with a weekend of food, entertainment, markets and plenty of Gallic flair around the harbour.

🏀 Mainland Pouākai vs Tauranga Whai

7:30pm | Parakiore Recreation and Sport Centre

Canterbury's professional women's basketball team takes on reigning champions Tauranga Whai — with 20% off tickets for ChchWeekly readers through the link above.

😂 Ben Hurley at Good Times Comedy Club

6:30pm | Good Times Comedy Club

One of New Zealand's best-known comedians is back in Christchurch with his solo stand-up show after a sold-out run earlier this year.

💦 Wipeout Waterpark

10:00am | Selwyn Aquatic Centre

Race, climb and splash your way across a giant indoor inflatable water obstacle course during the final stretch of the school holidays.

⛳ Hagley Hole-in-One

10:00am | North Hagley Park

Take a swing at the ultimate golf shot, win prizes and help raise funds for local men's wellbeing charity The Yarns Men.

Saturday, 10th Oct

🛍️ Christchurch Women's Lifestyle Expo

10:00am–4:00pm | Wolfbrook Arena

More than 170 exhibitors bring together food, fashion, beauty, wellness, homewares, gifts and plenty more for a massive two-day lifestyle expo.

🎪 The Extravaganza Fair

9:00am | The Red Zone, Burwood

The travelling fair rolls back into Christchurch with market stalls, street food, rides, live music, performers and old-school family fun.

🦁 Orana's 50th Birthday

10:00am | Orana Wildlife Park

Orana Wildlife Park celebrates its 50th birthday with special activities and entertainment throughout the park alongside the usual wildlife encounters.

🍺 Pomeroys 25 Year Celebration

12:00pm | Pomeroy's Old Brewery Inn

Pomeroy's marks 25 years with a big birthday celebration featuring specially brewed Canterbury beers, food and live entertainment.

🧶 Celebration of Fibrecraft

10:00am–3:00pm | Christchurch Bridge Club - Gold coin koha

See weaving, spinning, knitting, crochet, felting and more in action, with traders, demonstrations and hands-on craft activities for kids.

Sunday, 11th Oct

💀 The Strange Halloween Market

12:00pm–3:00pm | Sydenham Underpass - $2 entry

Halloween arrives early with alternative local stalls, trick-or-treating, coffin making, a costume competition, piñata and plenty of weird and wonderful finds. Kids under 12 are free.

⚡ Electrify Christchurch Expo

9:30am | Wigram Museum - Free

Check out electric cars, boats, tractors, solar, heat pumps and other technology changing how Kiwis travel and power their homes.

🦋 Butterfly Fun Run 2026

9:00am | The Groynes

Run or walk a 6km loop around The Groynes while supporting Baby Loss Awareness Week.

🇳🇱 Monthly Dutch Market

10:00am | Netherlands Society Cultural Centre - Free

Get a taste of the Netherlands with Dutch cheeses, herring, poffertjes, stroopwafels, oliebollen, crafts and plenty more.

🧘 Free Breathwork & Meditation Session

11:00am | Multicultural Recreation and Community Centre - Free

Take some time to slow down with a free guided breathing, meditation and yoga session designed to help you unwind and reset.

Christchurch and Canterbury are featuring in Season 39 of The Amazing Race, with hometown host Phil Keoghan bringing the show back to showcase the rebuilt city to millions of viewers overseas.

Residents have passed a vote of no confidence over plans for a major Te Awaparahi Bay expansion, calling for greater transparency around the project and its business case.
