Local headlines 📰

A couple of Christchurch stories you might’ve missed.

  • 🚆 Christchurch commuter rail trial receives $3.4m backing

    Commuter trains could be running between Rangiora, Rolleston and central Christchurch by mid-2027, following a $3.4 million government loan announcement. The proposed 12-month trial still needs funding commitments from local councils before it can go ahead.

  • 🍔 New McDonald's approved for Halswell

    McDonald's has been granted resource consent for a new restaurant at The Acres shopping precinct, next to Pak'nSave in Halswell. Plans include a drive-through and 18 car parks, with a possible opening in mid-to-late 2027.

  • 🛍️ The Body Shop is returning to Christchurch!

    After closing its New Zealand stores last year, The Body Shop is making a comeback at Westfield Riccarton. The new locally operated store will bring back its popular skincare, fragrances and body care products, with an opening expected soon.
