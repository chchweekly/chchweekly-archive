What's happening this weekend? 👀

8:30am–5:00pm | Canterbury Agricultural Park - From $15

The final day of Canterbury's biggest agricultural show, with animals, competitions, food and entertainment.

9:00am–5:00pm | Burwood Red Zone - Gold coin entry

A colourful travelling fair with live music, street food, quirky characters and kids' activities.

Gates 11:00am | Addington Raceway - Gold coin donation

Enjoy an afternoon of harness racing and entertainment to round out Cup Week.

Upcoming big events 🏟️

Big Christchurch events worth knowing about early.

🍷 South Island Wine & Food Festival

Sat 5 Dec 2026 | North Hagley Park

A celebration of the South Island's best food, wine and music! Sample wines from around the region, enjoy delicious food and catch live performances from Kora, Ladi6 and Flamingo Pier. Tickets are on sale now.

🎹 A Tribute to Carole King & James Taylor

Sun 14 Mar 2027 | James Hay Theatre

Just announced! A tribute celebrating the timeless music of Carole King and James Taylor is coming to Christchurch next March. The afternoon concert pays homage to two of music's most beloved singer-songwriters, with tickets going on sale this week.

🎶 Twominds Festival 2027

Thu 11 – Sun 14 Mar 2027 | Spencer Beach

Twominds has just revealed its huge fifth-anniversary lineup! L.A.B, The Black Seeds, Troy Kingi and nearly 100 other artists are heading to Spencer Beach for four days of live music and electronic beats. Tickets went on sale Wednesday evening!

Local headlines 📰

🚌 Metrocard and cash payments ending soon

From 23 November, Metrocard and cash will no longer be accepted on Greater

Christchurch buses and ferries. Passengers will need to switch to Motu Move or contactless payment.

⚡ Canterbury power bills could rise $21 a month

A proposed $1.3 billion upgrade to Orion's electricity network could add around $21 a month to the average Canterbury power bill by 2032. A final decision is expected next March.

🏥 Christchurch lands major international conference

Christchurch will host a major international pneumonia research conference in 2028, bringing around 1,000 health experts from across the world to Te Pae.
