Local Lore 📜

A little piece of Christchurch you might not know.

Hagley Park's Blossoms are almost 100 Years Old 🌸

The Yoshino cherry trees along Harper Avenue were planted in 1936. Nearly 90 years later, they’re still one of the first signs that spring has arrived in Christchurch.
